Education is my passion. My guiding principle has been to support and uplift others. This personal mission fuels my daily efforts to empower individuals in their educational endeavors and professional growth.

Dr. Chandra Sekhar Pemmasani

Founder & CEO, UWorld

Dr. Chandra Sekhar Pemmasani, M.D., is the founder and CEO of UWorld. At age 25, he founded USMLEWorld (rebranded as UWorld). What started in a university dorm room has since evolved into a leading force in education that has helped millions of students achieve their educational and career dreams. In addition to medical, UWorld now offers products in accounting, finance, graduate school, high school, nursing, and pharmacy, with additional products under development.

 

The idea for UWorld originated during Dr. Pemmasani’s medical residency, in response to requests from several colleagues seeking help studying for their entrance exams. After writing a 200-page review and study guide for the US Medical Licensing Examination (USMLE), Dr. Pemmasani took the manuscript to several publishers, who advised him to return when he had completed his medical training. Dr. Pemmasani turned instead to the internet and developed several online review products while maintaining his full residency schedule.

Awards & Recognition

UWorld, has been included in the GSV 150, a list of the world’s most transformative private companies in education. UWorld was chosen from over 4,000 venture capital and private equity-backed private companies revolutionizing the world of education technology.

Dr. Chandra Pemmasani, was selected as a winner of the EY Entrepreneur Of The Year® Southwest Region Award. Entrepreneur Of The Year is one of the preeminent competitive business awards for entrepreneurs and leaders of high-growth companies. The Southwest program celebrates entrepreneurs from North Texas, Arkansas, Oklahoma, and Wichita, Kansas.

UWorld is the winner of two Parent and Teacher Choice™ Awards from HowtoLearn.com for its ACT and SAT College Prep tools.The Parent and Teacher Choice Awards from HowtoLearn.com are one of the most recognized international awards by both parents and teachers.

Dr. Chandra Sekhar Pemmasani, the visionary Founder and CEO of UWorld, LLC, stands as a beacon of innovation and leadership in the education technology sector. With a profound commitment to transforming the learning experience, Pemmasani has steered UWorld to remarkable heights.
