Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What topics are covered in the NAPLEX Rapid Review?

Our NAPLEX Rapid Review focuses on the most critical, high-yield content to ensure you are exam-ready. The 20 essential topics covered include:

  • Cardiovascular & Renal: Hypertension, Chronic Heart Failure, Dyslipidemia, Anticoagulation, and Renal Disease.
  • Calculations, Biostatistics & Compounding Mixed Calculations, Biostatistics, and Compounding.
  • Pulmonary & Endocrine: Asthma, COPD, Diabetes & Thyroid
  • Infectious Diseases I-IV and HIV
  • Others including: Osteoporosis, Oncology, Pain, Seizures, and Depression/Anxiety

How do I use the NAPLEX Rapid Review?

To ensure you are fully prepared for exam day, follow this four-step success plan designed by our experts:

  1. Strengthen Your Foundation: Start with our foundational questions and topic review videos to gauge your understanding of core pharmacy principles.
  2. Master Exam-Style Logic: Practice with realistic, NAPLEX-style questions to build the clinical stamina and reasoning skills required for the boards.
  3. Learn from the Experts: Watch our step-by-step question explainer videos to see exactly how to dissect NAPLEX questions and avoid common exam traps.
  4. Assess Your Readiness: Complete the full 80-item NAPLEX practice exam to simulate the testing environment and confirm you are ready to pass.

How long should I prepare using the NAPLEX Rapid Review?

The Rapid Review is designed to be flexible, allowing you to choose between an intensive sprint or a steady, long-term study plan during your 90-day access period. Depending on your exam date and current comfort level, you can choose the path that best fits your needs:

  • 3–5 Day Intensive Review: This is ideal if your exam is only a few weeks away and you need a high-yield, fast-paced refresher. Plan to spend 8 or more hours a day in a quiet space, completing the full review before taking the 80-item practice exam as a final simulation.
  • 90-Day Flexible Review: This is the best fit if you have more time before your test date or feel unsure about certain topics. Tackle one topic at a time, use the foundational questions to identify your weak spots, and fill in any knowledge gaps using your full NAPLEX Online Course.

When should I take the NAPLEX?

We recommend taking the NAPLEX within 90 days of your graduation to ensure you pass on your first attempt. Testing during this window allows you to capitalize on “content freshness,” as the clinical knowledge and guidelines you mastered in pharmacy school are still top-of-mind.
